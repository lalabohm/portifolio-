I am a member of IDP's IEEE Student Branch, also active in the branch's
IES (Industry Engagement Sector) and WIE (Women in Engineering) groups.
Below are the branch events and activities I took part in.

## CTF Workshop

I took part in the CTF Workshop, an introduction to cybersecurity and to
Capture The Flag challenges. The workshop covered practical tips and
strategies for cryptography, web, logic, programming, networking and
digital forensics challenges, with Enzo Teles (S3r4ph1el), an offensive
security analyst specialized in pentesting and Red Team, who shared his
experience competing in CTFs across Brazil.
[See post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ieee-student-branch-idp_workshop-de-ctf-que-introdu%C3%A7%C3%A3o-incr%C3%ADvel-activity-7496960169139142656-2ucq?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAEXYqU8BQmzGNuTXu5GroCkUHA1nIMTN7e0)

## IEEE Cybersecurity Hackathon 2026

I competed on the IEEE IDP team at the IEEE Cybersecurity Hackathon 2026,
an international competition that brought together 190 participants from
34 countries to develop cybersecurity and artificial intelligence
solutions. We placed **2nd in the Capture The Flag** among 25 teams, which
qualified us for the final round with the top 10 groups. In the final
round we presented [Pangeia](../../projects/pangeia-adversarial-defense/),
a solution to harden machine-learning-based DDoS detection systems, and
reached **4th place overall in the IEEE Computer Society track**.
[See post](https://lnkd.in/p/dk-6PHk4)

## Talk “CYBERATTACKS: Understanding and Fighting Ransomware”

I attended the talk by Rafael Salema Marques (SWaNk), hosted by the
branch, on how ransomware attacks work, their real-world impact, defense
strategies and tools, and the importance of ethics in cyberspace. The
speaker shared real experiences from the field, including developing an
iOS ransomware that caught McAfee's attention.
[See post](https://lnkd.in/p/d3gBn3K4)

## Image Processing Workshop

I took part in the Image Processing Workshop, held on October 20 and 21
and led by Professor Rafael Paes. In the workshop I studied the
fundamentals of computer vision and image processing and analysis
techniques, with applications to satellite imagery, sports and everyday
scenes.
[See post](https://lnkd.in/p/d3-PkJrK)
