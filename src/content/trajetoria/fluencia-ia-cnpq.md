Training to develop skills in using generative artificial intelligence
systems effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely, including the
supervised use of AI agents in public-sector work.

The course covered:

- using *skills* to support the analysis of research funding processes
  under Brazil's Legal Framework for Science, Technology and Innovation;
- compliance with CNPq Ordinance No. 2,664/2026 on the use of generative AI
  in research;
- applying Brazil's data protection law (LGPD) to research funding;
- hands-on analysis exercises with fictitious proposals;
- converting documents into structured text before using them with
  language models.
